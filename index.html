    Loans and debt schedule

    EMI = P·r·(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1) on monthly rest, rounded to the nearest rupee. Reduce tenure keeps the EMI fixed so every prepayment shortens the loan. Reduce EMI recalculates your EMI after each prepayment to hold the original close date — ongoing extras can still close it earlier. Extra-monthly and step-up payments are treated as prepayments toward principal. No processing fees, insurance or charges are modelled. Defaults mirror your sheet: ₹75 L at 7.8% for 29 years with a ₹59,196 year-end prepayment (your schedule's actual 0.65%/month rate — the Setup tab's “8%” label is rounded).
